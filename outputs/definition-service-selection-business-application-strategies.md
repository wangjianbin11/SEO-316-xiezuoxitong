## Risk Mitigation in Global Supply Chains

The global landscape is volatile. Therefore, your **definition service selection business application strategies** must include contingency planning. Relying on a single point of failure—whether it is a single factory or a single shipping route—is a recipe for disaster. [Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2020/09/global-supply-chains-in-a-post-pandemic-world) suggests that 'regionalization' and 'multi-sourcing' are the keys to surviving modern disruptions. 

We advise our clients to select services that offer 'Dynamic Routing.' This means if one carrier is delayed, the system automatically selects the next best option based on real-time data. This level of sophistication in your business application prevents the dreaded 'Your package is stuck' emails that kill customer lifetime value. 

Additionally, consider the geographical diversity of your service providers. Having access to both China-based sourcing and local [US/EU warehousing](https://asgdropshipping.com/global-warehousing) allows you to balance cost and speed. This hybrid approach is a hallmark of sophisticated service selection. It allows you to stay lean during testing phases and move fast once you find a winner.
